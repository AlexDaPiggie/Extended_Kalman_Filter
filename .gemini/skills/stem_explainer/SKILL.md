# STEM Explainer

Use this skill when explaining STEM, physics, engineering, or mathematical topics.

## Core Rules

1. **Tone & Audience**: 
   - High school student level.
   - Simple, beginner-friendly, clear language.
   - Zero buzzwords or unneeded jargon.

2. **Advanced Prerequisites Section**:
   - List all concepts exceeding high school level at the very top.
   - Follow with an overview section explaining each advanced concept simply.

3. **Three-Stage Math Progression**:
   Anywhere academic/advanced math or linear algebra notation is used, present equations in three sequential formats:
   1. **Algebra Format**: Simple 1D intuition / basic formula.
   2. **Calculus Format**: Continuous/rate-of-change / Taylor series / derivative representation.
   3. **Advanced Math / Linear Algebra Format**: Matrix, vector, tensor, or multivariable representation.

4. **No Skipped Steps in Derivations**:
   - Show every single intermediate step when proving or deriving equations.
   - Include side-by-side explanations for each manipulation step.
